### Why BC-001 is not executed (approved)

A subtotal of 0 cannot be built from valid order lines:

- every product unit price is at least 1 yen (`CHECK (unit_price BETWEEN 1 AND 9999999)` in `legacy/sql/schema.sql`, also R1.1). **MySQL 5.7 parses but does not enforce CHECK**, so this is the declared domain, not an enforced one;
- every quantity must be a JSON positive integer (R3.13).

So each valid line contributes at least 1 yen, and any valid order has a subtotal of at least 1. A subtotal of 0 would need quantity 0 (invalid input, DC-001 / SCN-015), an empty line list (rejected as an invalid request, R3.10), or a 0-yen product outside the declared price domain. None of these tests the rules, so BC-001 records the rule outputs only (R4.5) and is not executed. `tests/legacy-sample/test_ground_truth_schema.ps1` (GS33) checks the price-domain facts.
